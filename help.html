Help & Support

Email us at support@emarket.com or call (000) 000-0000, Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm.

Since we are a peer-to-peer marketplace, shipping or pickup arrangements are made directly between the buyer and the seller. For local meetups, we recommend meeting in a well-lit, public locations. If a seller offers shipping, please request a tracking number directly from them once the item is sent.

All transactions are private agreements between the buyer and seller. We do not process payments and cannot issue refunds. We encourage buyers to thoroughly inspect items in person before completing a sale. If you encounter a fraudulent seller or a misrepresented item, contact us.

We do not process payments on our site. Users typically use cash, Venmo, or PayPal. Be wary of sellers who demand payment via non-refundable methods before you have seen the item. Always complete the transaction through a method that offers buyer protection when possible.
